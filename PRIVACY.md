# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

BADHON-X runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit,
store on any server, log, or share any personal information.

## What BADHON-X does with your data

- Your GitHub token is stored only on your phone, in
  ~/.config/badhon-x/config.json with file permissions 600.
- It is used only to talk to api.github.com on your behalf.
- Your repositories are downloaded directly from GitHub into the storage
  folder you choose and stay on your device.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no third-party services.

## What BADHON-X does not do

- It does not send your token, username, repositories or files anywhere
  other than the official GitHub API.
- It does not read files that are not part of the repositories you own.
- It does not modify, delete, or push anything back to GitHub.
  It only reads.

## Your rights

- You can delete your token anytime with: badhon-x --logout
- You can delete all stored data by removing the storage folder you chose
  and the folder ~/.config/badhon-x
- You can audit the source code. It is short, plain Python and fully public.

If you have any privacy question, open an issue on the repository.
